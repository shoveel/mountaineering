LIFE AT THE TOP · 05

STUDENT 3 (about 30 seconds) How does the food chain work in the mountains? It looks like a mountain too. At the bottom there are lots of plants: grasses, mosses and lichens. Herbivores, like pikas, marmots and ibex, eat the plants. They are the prey. At the top there are only a few predators, like the snow leopard and the puma, that hunt the herbivores. Energy goes up the mountain. Then scavengers, like condors and vultures, eat the leftovers, and what is left goes back to the soil. In the photo, a snow leopard is guarding the prey it caught.
