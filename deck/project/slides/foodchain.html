LIFE AT THE TOP · 05

STUDENT 3 (about 30 seconds) How does the food chain work in the mountains? It starts with plants: grasses, mosses, lichens and flowers. Herbivores, like pikas, marmots, blue sheep and ibex, eat these plants. They are the prey. Then predators, like the snow leopard, the puma and the golden eagle, hunt the herbivores. Finally, scavengers like condors, vultures and magpies eat what is left. You can see this in the photo: the snow leopard caught its prey, and the magpie is waiting for the leftovers.
