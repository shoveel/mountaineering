Threats

What puts mountain wildlife in danger?

01

Climate change

Glaciers are melting. Animals move higher to stay cool, until there is nowhere higher to go.

02

Poaching

Snow leopards are hunted illegally for their fur and bones.

03

Conflict with people

When predators kill farm animals, some herders kill them back. Condors die from poisoned bait and lead from bullets.

04

Losing their home

Mines, roads and too many farm animals destroy habitats and eat the grass wild animals need.

STUDENT 4 (about 30 seconds) Unfortunately, mountain animals are in danger. The biggest threat is climate change. Glaciers, like the one in this photo in Nepal, are melting, and animals have to move higher to find cold places, until there is nowhere higher to go. Snow leopards are also killed by poachers for their fur and bones. Sometimes herders kill predators that attack their animals, and condors die when they eat poisoned meat or meat with lead from bullets. Mines, roads and too many farm animals also destroy their homes.
