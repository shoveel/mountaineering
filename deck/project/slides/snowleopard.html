LIFE AT THE TOP · 06

STUDENT 3 (about 35 seconds) Our first animal is the snow leopard. People call it the “ghost of the mountains” because it is very hard to see. It lives between 3,000 and 5,500 metres, and there are only about 4,000 to 6,500 left in the wild. Let’s look at its body. Its big nose warms the icy air, and big lungs get more oxygen. Its fur is very thick, up to 12 centimetres on its belly. Its grey, spotted coat is perfect camouflage on rocks. Its tail is about one metre long: it helps with balance, and it works like a scarf when it sleeps. And its wide, furry paws work like snowshoes.
