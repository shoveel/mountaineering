LIFE AT THE TOP · 06

STUDENT 3 (about 35 seconds) Our first animal is the snow leopard. People call it the “ghost of the mountains” because it is very hard to see. It lives between 3,000 and 5,500 metres, and there are only about 4,000 to 6,500 left in the wild. How does it survive? It has very thick fur, up to 12 centimetres long on its belly. Its tail is about one metre long: it helps the leopard balance on rocks, and it uses it like a scarf when it sleeps. Its big paws work like snowshoes. And its wide nose and big lungs help it breathe the cold, thin air. Its grey, spotted coat is also great camouflage on rocky slopes.
