▲ SUMMIT · 8,849 M

LIFE AT THE TOP · 10

STUDENT 5 (about 25 seconds) We made it to the summit! Here are some fun facts. An Andean condor once flew 172 kilometres in five hours without flapping its wings even once. Snow leopards can’t roar: they make a soft sound called a “chuff”. In Italy, Alpine ibex climb almost vertical dam walls to lick salt from the stones. And mountains are home to more than 85 percent of the world’s species of birds, mammals and amphibians.
