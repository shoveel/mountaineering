▲ SUMMIT · 8,849 M

LIFE AT THE TOP · 10

STUDENT 5 (about 25 seconds) We made it to the summit! Here are some fun facts. Condors are amazing gliders: they ride warm air for hours and hardly flap their wings. Snow leopards can’t roar like lions: they purr and make a soft sound called a “chuff”. Ibex and mountain goats are expert climbers that can walk up almost vertical rock. And mountains are like water towers: many of the world’s great rivers start as snow and ice high in the mountains.
