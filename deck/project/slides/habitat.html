LIFE AT THE TOP · 03

STUDENT 2 (about 30 seconds) What makes mountains special? The higher you go, the colder it gets: about six and a half degrees colder for every thousand metres. The air is also thinner. At five thousand five hundred metres, every breath has only half the oxygen. Mountains have zones: forests at the bottom, meadows above the tree line, and rock and ice at the top. Life here must also survive strong winds, strong sunlight, poor soil and very short summers.
