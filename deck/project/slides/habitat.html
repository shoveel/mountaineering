LIFE AT THE TOP · 03

STUDENT 2 (about 30 seconds) What makes mountains special? Look at this drawing. Mountains have zones: forest at the bottom, then alpine meadows above the tree line, then bare rock, and snow and ice at the top. The higher you go, the colder it gets: about six and a half degrees colder for every thousand metres, so it can be minus 32 degrees at the top of Everest. The air is also thinner. The dots show the oxygen: at 5,500 metres there is only half the air. Above 8,000 metres is the “death zone”, where people can’t survive for long.
