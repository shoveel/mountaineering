Protected areas

National parks, like the one in the photo, keep habitats safe.

02

Safe corrals

Covered pens protect farm animals at night, so herders don’t kill snow leopards.

03

Stop poaching

Park rangers, strong laws and no buying or selling of fur and bones.

04

No poison, no lead

Lead-free bullets and no poisoned bait keep the condor’s food safe.

05

Breed and release

Zoos raise condor chicks and release them back into the wild.

06

Fight climate change

Save energy, walk or cycle, and waste less. Small actions add up.

STUDENT 5 (about 30 seconds) So, what can we do? First, protected areas, like national parks, keep habitats safe. Second, covered corrals protect farm animals at night, so herders don’t need to kill snow leopards. Third, we need park rangers and strong laws against poaching. Fourth, hunters should use lead-free bullets and nobody should use poison, so condors can eat safely. Some zoos also raise condor chicks and release them into the wild. And all of us can help fight climate change: save energy, walk or cycle more, and waste less.
