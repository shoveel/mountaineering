LIFE AT THE TOP · 09

STUDENT 5 (about 30 seconds) So, what can we do? First, protected areas, like national parks, keep habitats safe. Second, covered corrals protect farm animals at night, so herders don’t need to kill snow leopards. Third, we need park rangers and strong laws against poaching. Fourth, nobody should use poison or lead bullets, so condors can eat safely. Fifth, some zoos raise condor chicks and release them into the wild. And finally, all of us can help cool the planet: save energy, walk or cycle more, and waste less.
