LIFE AT THE TOP · 04

STUDENT 2 (about 25 seconds) Even with all these difficulties, many animals live in the mountains. In the Himalayas there are snow leopards, yaks and red pandas. The Andes have condors, pumas, and guanacos, like the one in this photo, a wild cousin of the llama. In the Alps you can see the Alpine ibex, which can walk on almost vertical rocks. And in the Rocky Mountains there are mountain goats, bighorn sheep and grizzly bears.
