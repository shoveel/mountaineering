STUDENT 2 (about 30 seconds) Even with all these difficulties, many animals live in the mountains. In the Himalayas there are snow leopards, red pandas and yaks, which have long, shaggy fur and huge lungs for the thin air. The Andes have condors, pumas, guanacos, like the one in this photo, and vicuñas, which grow some of the finest wool in the world. In the Alps you can see the Alpine ibex and marmots, which sleep underground for up to six months in winter. And in the Rocky Mountains there are mountain goats, grizzly bears and pikas, small animals that collect grass all summer and store it like hay.
