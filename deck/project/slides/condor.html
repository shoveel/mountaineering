LIFE AT THE TOP · 07

STUDENT 4 (about 30 seconds) Our second animal is the Andean condor. It is one of the largest flying birds on Earth: its wings can be 3.3 metres wide, and it can weigh up to 15 kilos. Its giant wings let it glide on warm air for hours, almost without flapping. It has no feathers on its head, so it stays clean when it eats. It has very sharp eyes to find dead animals from the sky. The condor is a scavenger, so it is nature’s clean-up crew: by eating dead animals, it helps stop diseases.
