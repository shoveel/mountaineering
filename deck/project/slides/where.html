LIFE AT THE TOP · 02

STUDENT 1 (about 30 seconds) Mountains are found on every continent. We chose four famous ranges. The Himalayas, in Asia, have the highest mountain in the world: Mount Everest, at 8,849 metres. The Andes, in South America, are the longest mountain range on any continent, about 7,000 kilometres. The Alps are in the middle of Europe, and the Rocky Mountains go from Canada to the south of the United States. The orange bars show how high the tallest peak of each range is.
